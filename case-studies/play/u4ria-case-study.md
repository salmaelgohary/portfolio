# U4RIA

**Role:** AI Content Designer & Developer · **Type:** Contract · **Timeline:** 3 months (September–November 2025) · **Skills:** Midjourney, Suno, Airtable, Kling AI, Prompt Engineering

#### Overview

## Building a repeatable AI content pipeline for a wellness app at scale

U4RIA is a lifestyle app that uses AI to generate meditations, music, recipes, and daily affirmations at scale. While AI makes asset creation fast, keeping a consistent brand look and feel across hundreds of pieces of content is a major hurdle. Without a structured workflow, every single output requires the same manual fixing as starting from scratch.

I took lead on designing and running the AI content pipeline, turning brand intent into a reliable system that produced every piece of content the app shipped with.

#### The Challenge

## Maintaining consistency at volume is hard

Generating one-and-done AI assets is straightforward, but keeping a large library of various types of content consistent in visuals and tone is not.

Without a system, the AI outputs drift all over the place and require constant revisions. To solve this, I decided to treat AI like a production line by writing clear instructions, testing what worked best, and turning those successful formulas into steps the team could repeat.

#### The Pipeline

## Five stages that made AI outputs reliable at volume

### Define intent first

Before opening any tool, I established the communication goal, emotional tone, and format requirements to anchor every output in the core brand direction.

### Translate intent into structure

Second, I converted creative direction into structured prompts for Midjourney, Suno, and Kling AI, iterating on the language to keep results steady and predictable.

### Generate and evaluate

Third, I produced multiple variations per prompt and selected the ones that hit the right balance of brand alignment, clarity, and quality.

### Refine for platform

Fourth, I brought generated assets into Canva to polish composition, pacing, typography, and platform-specific sizing for Instagram, TikTok, and in-app placements.

### Manage at scale

Finally, I built an organized tracking system in Airtable to log prompt formulas, ensuring asset sets remained cohesive while leaving room for intentional variety.

#### Outcomes

## Reduced revision work by 38%

Setting goals early and reusing tracked prompts meant way less going back and forth trying to get things right.

#### Reflection

## What I learned

### Specificity is a design constraint

AI works best when your prompts are clear boundaries instead of vague suggestions. When you clearly define the mood, inputs, and rules right at the start, you get fewer variant results and everything moves a lot faster.

### AI is an execution layer, not a decision maker.

I learned to make creative and strategic decisions before involving AI. Treating AI as a production tool, not a source of direction, resulted in higher-quality, more intentional outputs and maintained creative control throughout.
